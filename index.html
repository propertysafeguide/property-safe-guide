The 4-Tier Texas Due Diligence System

Click any tier below to explore the exact checks, verified data sources, and critical red flags built directly into our interactive system.

Performed before spending fuel or booking trips to the acreage. Establishes baseline geographical and legal boundaries.

Primary Verification Checklist:

  • FEMA Floodplain Overlay (Zone X vs Zone AE/A)
  • County Appraisal District (CAD) GIS Boundary overlay
  • Web Soil Survey (USDA septic suitability rating)
  • Texas Water Development Board (TWDB) aquifer maps

Crimson Red Flag Alerts:

  • More than 30% of buildable envelope in 100-year flood zone
  • USDA "Very Limited" soil rating for conventional septic
  • CAD boundary mismatch exceeding 15 feet along access road

Review official Texas County Clerk filings to verify ownership chains, encumbrances, and tax liabilities.

Primary Verification Checklist:

  • Mineral Reservation Clauses (Severed mineral rights check)
  • Pipeline & Electrical Ingress/Egress Easement documents
  • Agricultural 1-D-1 Valuation status and history
  • Access deed verification (Deeded legal access guarantee)

Crimson Red Flag Alerts:

  • 100% Mineral Estate severance with surface lease waivers missing
  • Pending 3-year Ag Valuation Rollback Tax penalty ($5,000–$25,000)
  • Access via unrecorded prescriptive easement or verbal agreement

On-site physical inspection checklist to measure physical utilities, topography, and real-world conditions.

Primary Verification Checklist:

  • Measure distance from build site to nearest electric pole
  • Neighboring well depth & flow logs (TWDB database cross-reference)
  • Cellular carrier strength test at primary build envelope
  • Fence line vs monument marker physical verification

Crimson Red Flag Alerts:

  • Electric pole distance > 500 ft (Electric Co-op drop fee: $10k–$25k)
  • Neighboring well depths exceeding 400 ft with low GPM flow
  • Unrecorded encroachments or active pipelines visible on-site

Mandatory TREC contract addenda and formal engineering/survey contingencies before earnest money goes non-refundable.

Primary Verification Checklist:

  • TREC Unimproved Property Contract special provisions rider
  • Licensed OSSF Site Evaluator septic percolation test
  • RPLS Category 1A Land Title Survey with corner pins set
  • Title Commitment Schedule B exception clause review

Crimson Red Flag Alerts:

  • Seller refusal to allow 30-day feasibility study period
  • Title commitment excluding standard survey exception coverage
  • OSSF Perc test requiring engineered aerobic system ($12k+)
